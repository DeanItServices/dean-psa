Build the surface a user lands on when they hold a temp password, so that the flag 07-03 sets can actually be cleared.

Output: New `src/app/(auth)/change-password/` — `page.tsx` (Server Component: session check and redirect), `change-password-form.tsx` (client form), and `actions.ts`.
</objective>
