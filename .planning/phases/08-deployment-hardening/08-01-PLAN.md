<objective>
Move the rate limiter and session gate off the `middleware.ts` convention Next.js 16
deprecated and onto `src/proxy.ts`, which runs on the Node runtime. This is the
prerequisite that makes 08-02's env-configurable limits genuine rather than illusory:
under Edge, `process.env` is inlined at build time.
</objective>
